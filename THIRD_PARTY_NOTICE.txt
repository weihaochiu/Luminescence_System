RisingCam SDK components
========================

This package includes nncam.py, nncam.dll, and x64 USB driver files taken from
the user-supplied RisingCam SDK archive, version 57.27250.20241216. Those files
remain third-party vendor components and are subject to the terms supplied by
their manufacturer.

Application dependencies are installed from PyPI:
- PySide6 (LGPLv3/GPLv3/commercial licensing options)
- Pillow (HPND License)
- NumPy (BSD-3-Clause License)
- tifffile (BSD-3-Clause License)
- OpenCV Python headless packages (Apache License 2.0)
- pytesseract (Apache License 2.0; requires a separately installed Tesseract OCR executable)
- PyVISA (MIT License)
- PyVISA-py (MIT License)
- PyUSB (BSD License)
